Your companion

It stays with you.

A small black cat who learns English with you. You give it a name. It remembers your words, the mistakes you make twice, and the moments you kept.

  • You name it.

    Momo, Pip, Nori. The name is yours, and it can change.

  • It remembers.

    Your words, your recurring mistakes, and the moments you saved.

  • It speaks first.

    A small line on the timeline, or the first message in Talk.

A character, not a tutor.

Privacy

Your words and moments are yours. The AI suggests; Named checks and saves. The model can change without changing your data.
