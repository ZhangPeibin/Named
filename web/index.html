Privacy

Private by design.

Your words and moments are yours. Named checks and saves; the model can change without changing your data.

12focused agents
6product surfaces
1shared runtime

  • Your data stays yours.

    Words, talks, and stories are account-scoped and deleted with your account.

  • Checked before saving.

    Agents return structured results. The backend validates them before they touch your data.

  • One model, replaceable.

    Twelve focused agents share one runtime. Swapping the model does not rewrite the product.
