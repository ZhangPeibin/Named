01 — The idea

Most tools teach words.
Milo starts from your life.

A word becomes yours when it is tied to something you saw, said, or felt. Milo begins with a question: what caught your eye today?

From there, Milo carries the word into your timeline, notebook, practice, and a story made from your own words.

One thread over and under: the new word tied to what you already know.

02 — Your companion
